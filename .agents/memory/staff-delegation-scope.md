---
name: Staff delegation boundaries
description: Why restricted coach/manager access is explicitly delegated per team and excludes shared event administration.
---

Staff access is an explicit, season-specific delegation from a full admin. Coach and Manager are descriptive labels, not automatic privileges; ordinary team membership and Notion must not grant staff access.

**Why:** The requested feature is granular access to specific teams, not another way to obtain the club's existing full-admin powers.

**How to apply:** Keep private identity/financial information and access administration with full admins. The first version deliberately leaves club-wide, group-based, and cross-team event administration with full admins, even when a coach manages every team represented in the audience. Expanding this requires an explicit permission and audience policy, not simply reusing a broader admin check.
