---
name: Manual invitee groups
description: Product scope for reusable event groups, independent of squads and World Cup history.
---

Groups are manually maintained member lists, not automatic membership rules.
They must allow mixing members across teams and including members who did not
attend the World Cup.

**Why:** The user needs to scope invitees for the ZOAT social game beyond the
single-squad restriction and explicitly chose manual member selection.

**How to apply:** Keep group membership independent of team assignment,
World Cup participation and Notion. Do not turn groups into rule-driven or
Notion-synchronized lists without a new user decision.
