---
name: Portal invitation semantics
description: Invitations notify selected members; they do not grant or restrict portal access.
---

Portal invitation selection controls who receives an email, not who is permitted to sign in. Keep email-code authentication independent of invitation history, membership category, team assignment, and Notion.

**Why:** The selective-invitation request replaces a Trials-only sending shortcut, not the existing active-member sign-in policy. Treating invitation delivery as an access grant would unexpectedly lock out other active members.

**How to apply:** Future invitation targeting, saved groups, resend controls and reporting must not turn invitation records into an authentication allowlist or alter event/squad eligibility. Pending or uncertain invitation deliveries follow the same no-automatic-retry principle as reminders.
