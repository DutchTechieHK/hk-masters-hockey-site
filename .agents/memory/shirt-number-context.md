---
name: Competition-specific shirt numbers
description: User-confirmed rule allowing different simultaneous numbers for league and tournament contexts.
---

Players can hold separate shirt numbers per competition simultaneously. Keep previous numbers and the context in which they were used, such as league versus tournaments.

**Why:** The user requested shirt-number history and explicitly selected separate numbers per competition rather than one current number overall.

**How to apply:** A change in one competition must not overwrite the number or historical record belonging to another competition.
